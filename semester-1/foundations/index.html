Algebra repair

Rearrange an equation by applying the same valid operation to both sides. Keep domain restrictions when dividing or taking roots. For products and fractions, simplify structure before inserting numbers.

Example

From y=mx+b with m≠0, x=(y−b)/m. If y=11, m=3 and b=2, then x=3.

Try it Solve 5x+2=17.

Check your answer

x=3. Subtract 2, then divide by 5.

Trigonometry repair

Resolve a vector relative to a stated angle. If θ is measured from the positive horizontal axis, x=A cos θ and y=A sin θ. Use radians for calculus derivative rules.

Example

A vector of magnitude 10 at 60° to the horizontal has horizontal component 5.

Try it Find the horizontal component of a magnitude-8 vector at 60°.

Check your answer

8 cos 60° = 4.

Graphs and functions

Read axes and units first. A slope is vertical change divided by horizontal change. A function value, slope and area represent different things. Check whether the question asks for an average over an interval or a local value.

Example

Points (1,3) and (4,9) give slope (9−3)/(4−1)=2.

Try it Find the slope through (2,5) and (6,13).

Check your answer

(13−5)/(6−2)=2.

Units and scientific notation

Multiply conversion factors equal to one so unwanted units cancel. Temperature in an ideal-gas ratio must use kelvin. Keep powers of ten and prefix conversions visible.

Example

0.025 L × 1000 mL/L = 25 mL. Also, 25°C = 298.15 K.

Try it Convert 0.15 kg to grams.

Check your answer

0.15 × 1000 = 150 g.

Exponents and logarithms

For positive a and b, ln(ab)=ln a+ln b. A logarithm reverses its matching exponential. These rules do not allow ln(a+b)=ln a+ln b.

Example

ln(e³)=3 and 10²=100. A logarithm input must be positive.

Try it Find log₁₀(1000).

Check your answer

3, because 10³=1000.
