Start with these candidate classes

These are original and concise reading objectives; These are not quotes or exercises from the book.

Scientific and chemical basis Chapters 1–3
1 · Learn Biology · p 1Read about the nature of science, hypotheses, and controlled experiments. Objective: Formulate a testable hypothesis and distinguish claim from evidence.
2 · Basic Chemistry of Biology · p 24Read atoms, bonds, water and pH. Objective: To relate the properties of molecules to cell functions.
3 · Molecules of life · p 42Read carbohydrates, fats, proteins and nucleic acids. Objective: Compare structure and function between the four categories.
Cell and energy | Chapters 4–7
4 · Cell tour · p 65Read types of cells, membranes and organelles. Objective: Link the structure of a component to its function.
5 · working cell · p 87Read ATP Enzymes and transmembrane transport, especially osmosis on p. 99. Objective: Predict the direction of movement of water and substances.
6 · Cellular respiration · p 106Read the overview, three phases, and glycolysis on p. 112. Objective: Trace energy and basic inputs and outputs.
7 · Photosynthesis · p 124Read about light reactions and the Calvin cycle. Objective: Explain the conversion of light energy into chemical energy.
Mitosis, heredity, and genetic information Chapters 8–10
8 · Cell cycle and division · p 142Read the phases of the cycle, mitosis, and cycle control. Objective: Arrange the stages and explain the role of the set point.
9 · inheritance patterns · p 168Read Mendel's law, Pant squares, and binary hybridization. Objective: Calculate the probability of a simple genotype or phenotype.
10 · Structure and function of DNA · p 196Read the structure of DNA and duplicate it, then copy and compile. Objective: Trace the transfer of information from DNA to RNA and then the protein.
