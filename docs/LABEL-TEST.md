# TEST A · LABEL TEST — navigation words only
8–12 Arabic speakers. 6 minutes each. Individually, never in a group.
Recruit people who do NOT know the project. Mix ages and reading habits.
At least 3 who rarely use apps.

RULE: the facilitator explains NOTHING before the test. Not the project, not the
Quran angle, not the word "تدبر". If a participant asks "what is this?", answer
"سأشرح بعد قليل" and continue.

## Materials
A card or screen showing ONLY these five words, in this order, no icons, no context:
    اللقاء · ما كتبتُه · ما اخترتُه · العودة · الأحزاب

## Part 1 — free interpretation (one at a time, uncovered in order)
For each word: "لو ضغطت هنا، ما الذي تتوقع أن تجد؟"
Write the answer VERBATIM. Do not paraphrase, do not help, do not confirm.
If they say "لا أعرف" — that is a result. Record it and move on.

## Part 2 — first-click tasks (show all five words at once)
Read each task once. Record the FIRST word they point to and the seconds taken.
  T1  "أين تكتب ما فهمته من آية قرأتها اليوم؟"                 → expected: اللقاء
  T2  "أين تقرأ ما كتبته سابقًا؟"                              → expected: ما كتبتُه
  T3  "أين تجد الشيء الذي قررت أن تعمل به؟"                   → expected: ما اخترتُه
  T4  "انقطعت أسبوعًا وتريد أن تسجّل ما حدث. أين تذهب؟"        → expected: العودة
  T5  "تريد أن تتصفح كل الآيات المتاحة. أين تذهب؟"             → expected: الأحزاب

## Part 3 — one comparison question (last, never first)
Show the OLD labels: اليوم · التدبر · ما أعمل عليه · مراجعتي · عودتي · ما يتكرر · رحلتي
"أي المجموعتين أوضح لك؟ ولماذا؟"
Record the reason, not just the choice. The reason is the finding.

## Success criteria (decide BEFORE running)
  PASS   ≥ 6 of 8 route T1–T5 correctly on first click, per task.
  REWRITE any single label that ≥ 3 participants route wrongly — INCLUDING the
         ones proposed in this project. No label is protected.
  RED FLAG if "اللقاء" is read as a meeting with a person, or "العودة" as
         "go back / رجوع". Both are plausible misreadings and both are fatal
         to the flow if real.

## What NOT to do
Do not defend a label. Do not say "it means…". Do not run this with anyone who
has seen the app. Do not let one person's answer be heard by the next.
