# TEST B · FIVE-PERSON ENCOUNTER PILOT — one H01 card, observed
5 participants. ~20 minutes each. In person if possible. One observer, one device.
NO instrumentation, NO analytics, NO recording of the screen. Pen and paper only.

Recruit: 5 adults who read Arabic comfortably and are not in the project.
Aim for at least 2 who would not describe themselves as "into apps",
and at least 2 who do read Quran regularly. Do not recruit only enthusiasts.

## Consent (say this verbatim)
"سأطلب منك أن تستخدم شاشة واحدة لبضع دقائق. لا يوجد صح وخطأ، ونحن نختبر الشاشة
لا نختبرك. ما تكتبه يبقى على هذا الجهاز، ولن نأخذ نسخة منه، ولن نصوّر الشاشة.
يمكنك التوقف في أي لحظة بلا سبب. هل توافق؟"
Delete the participant's writing in front of them at the end (menu → مسح بياناتي).

## Facilitator script — read exactly, add nothing
  1. "افتح هذه الشاشة وتعامل معها كما لو كنت وحدك." → hand over the device. STOP TALKING.
  2. Say NOTHING for the next 3 minutes, whatever happens. Silence is the instrument.
  3. If they ask what to do: "ما الذي تتوقع أنت أنه المطلوب؟" then silence again.
  4. If they stall for more than 60 seconds: "ما الذي يمنعك الآن؟" then silence.
  5. When they stop or say they are done: "هل انتهيت؟" — and let them stop there.

## What NOT to explain — before or during
  ✗ what تدبر means, or that this is a reflection app
  ✗ that the chips are optional
  ✗ that "لا" is an acceptable answer to the bridge question
  ✗ that the tools panel is not part of the ayah
  ✗ the project, the 60 hizbs, the research, or your intentions
  ✗ anything that would make a confusing screen make sense
Every explanation you give destroys the finding you came for.

## Questions to ask AFTER, in this order (never before)
  Q1 "بكلماتك، ما الذي كانت هذه الشاشة تطلبه منك؟"
  Q2 "هل شعرت أن هناك جوابًا صحيحًا مطلوبًا منك؟"            ← the key question
  Q3 "حين ظهرت الكلمات الصغيرة (إدراك، وجهة…) هل غيّرت ما كتبته أو ما كنت ستكتبه؟"
  Q4 "حين سُئلت هل مسّ المعنى شيئًا في حياتك — كيف شعرت بالسؤال؟"
  Q5 "لو أجبت بـ«لا»، هل كنت ستشعر أنك أخفقت؟"
  Q6 "ما الذي كان مزعجًا أو غير واضح؟"
  Q7 "هل كنت ستفتح هذه الشاشة مرة أخرى غدًا؟ ولماذا؟"       ← accept a "no" gratefully
  Q8 "هل هناك شيء توقعته ولم تجده؟"

## Success / failure criteria — decide BEFORE running
  PASS      ≥ 3 of 5 write something unprompted, in their own words, without asking
            what is required.
  PASS      ≥ 4 of 5 answer Q2 with "لا" (no right answer was felt).
  PASS      ≥ 4 of 5 say a "لا" to the bridge would not have felt like failure.
  WARNING   ≥ 2 of 5 revise their writing after seeing the chips
            → progressive disclosure is not sufficient; the chips move later or go.
  FAIL      ≥ 3 of 5 cannot say what the screen asked of them (Q1).
  FAIL      any participant reads the tools panel as part of the ayah's meaning.
  FAIL      any participant experiences the bridge as pressure to produce an action.
  HARD STOP any participant becomes distressed → end the session, stay with them,
            offer the numbers in "تحتاج مساعدة الآن؟", and do not continue the pilot
            that day.

## Reporting rule
Write the five sheets up WITHOUT aggregating into a percentage. Five people are
five people. Quote them. Resist the urge to compute anything.
