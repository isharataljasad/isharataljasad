# Geometry episode 02 teaching sample

The original Points, Lines and Planes chapter was expanded as one representative teaching sample. The other six chapters and the episode inventory were not expanded. The sample is original prose and original SVG illustrations, with no publisher transcript or artwork reproduced.

## Teaching scope

The explanation covers ideal objects, dimensions, segments and rays, naming, collinearity and coplanarity, unique determination, counting objects without duplicate names, intersections, line-plane cases, skew lines, plane-plane cases, interpreting drawings, sketching from statements, and engineering modeling limits. It is an introductory incidence-geometry chapter, not the whole Geometry curriculum.

Four diagrams illustrate extents, one consistent named configuration, multiple planes through one line, and the three line-plane cases. Twelve question families follow the coverage summary. Twelve native HTML answer disclosures provide reasoning without requiring JavaScript or a quiz score. They are representative question families, not every possible question.
