## Corrections to the old material

- Remove the assumption that every visitor already has IELTS 7. These foundations do not assign a validated entry level.

- Remove attempt/check gates and hidden-answer flows from the reading-only experience.

- Active voice can be appropriate in academic work. Passive voice is not automatically more accurate, and past passive is appropriate for many completed procedures.

- The verb “put” is not inherently slang; choose a more precise verb when the action requires it.

- Replace mistranslations such as “currents” for material streams and “geometry” where scientific measurement is intended.

- Do not claim that a Pearson-branded technical-English curriculum was supplied when the local file describes only a style-based original route.

## Deliberate remaining limits

- No complete IELTS test series, coverage of every task type, timed examination, grading or score guarantee.

- No recorded listening, pronunciation demonstration, speaking assessment or calibrated progression across CEFR levels.

- Map-description variants, extensive listening spelling practice, extended IELTS speaking formats, advanced research/abstract writing and professional safety communication remain outside this foundation package.

- Grammar coverage is a selected bridge, not an exhaustive reference grammar. Advanced parallelism and rhetorical analysis require further material.

- University course alignment and the audience’s starting level remain unconfirmed. Independent language-teacher review and learner feedback have not yet occurred.
