Units and mass fractions

Before any material balance you need two tools: a consistent unit for each quantity, and a mass fraction that you know what it means. Most errors in engineering calculations are not in algebra, but in adding two numbers with two different units, or in multiplying a flow by a percentage without dividing it by a hundred.

Used directly in: material balance lesson — Everything here is used directly there: Component flow = Total flow × mass fraction.

1 · Quantity is not a number alone

Engineering value = number + unit. “The flow 150» is an incomplete sentence that cannot be verified. «Flow 150 kg/h» complete sentence.

From this follows the rule of addition: Add quantities only after expressing them in the same unit. Combining 12 mm with 1 cm does not equal 13; One of them is converted first to : 12 mm + 10 mm = 22 mm.

From this follows the multiplication rule: units are multiplied and divided with numbers. If you multiply kg/h by an hour you get kg. The unit itself tells you if the calculation is reasonable.

2 · Conversion by multiplying by one

Do not save the conversion direction. Write the conversion factor as a fraction of one, then multiply so that the unwanted unit is abbreviated.

Example: convert 0.5 t/h To kg/h. We know that 1 t = 1000 kgThe breakage (1000 kg / 1 t) Its value is one.

0.5 t/h × (1000 kg / 1 t) = 500 kg/h. The ton was abbreviated because it was in the numerator and denominator.

If you multiply the fraction upside down, (1 t / 1000 kg)When something was shortened: (t/h) × (t/kg) = t²/(h·kg). The ton was multiplied by itself and became Squared in the numeratorThe kilogram remains in the denominator, and this unit has no meaning here. The resulting unit is what tells you that you hit in the right directionNot your memory.

3 · Mass fraction: precise definition

The mass fraction of an ingredient is the mass of that ingredient divided by the mass of the whole mixture.

x = Component mass ÷ Mixture mass

Because it is a mass-to-mass ratio, it is.. dimensionless, and its value is always between zero and one. The sum of the fractions of all the components in one stream equals exactly one, because you divided the whole by the whole.

The percentage is the fraction itself multiplied by one hundred dissolved. «20% means x = 0.20. In calculations, use fractions, not ratios. The percentage is for display only.

4 · The relationship that is used in every budget

From the definition of mass fraction comes the relationship that you will write in each balancing problem:

Component flow = Total flow × Mass fraction

Note the units: kg/h × (dimensionless) = kg/h. The result is flow, not ratio. Here's a quick check: If you get a unitless number where you're expecting a flow, you've hit something wrong.

Conversely, if you know the component flow and the total flow, the fraction is the result of dividing them, and the units are abbreviated and an abstract number comes out as it should.

6 · Application

Try before you open the answer. The answers explain the cause, not the effect alone.

  1. Convert 1.2 t/h To kg/h, writing the conversion factor.

    The answer and its reason

    1.2 t/h × (1000 kg / 1 t) = 1200 kg/h. The ton was shortened, so it remained kg/h It is required.

  2. Stream 250 kg/h Mass fraction of salt 0.04. What is the flow of salt and the flow of water?

    The answer and its reason

    Salt: 250 × 0.04 = 10 kg/h. Water: 250 × 0.96 = 240 kg/h. Examination: 10 + 240 = 250 kg/h.

  3. A stream containing 30 kg/h solute and its total flow is 120 kg/h. What is the mass fraction and percentage?

    The answer and its reason

    30 ÷ 120 = 0.25 Unitless, i.e. 25%. The units are abbreviated because the two quantities flow.

  4. Your colleague said that the fraction of a component in a stream equals 1.3. what is wrong without seeing his calculation?

    The answer and its reason

    A fraction is part of a whole, so it cannot exceed one. It is more likely that he divided by the mass of the ingredient instead of the mass of the mixture, or added ratios from two different streams.

  5. A stream of A has the amount of 100 kg/h and its fraction is 0.20, and a stream of B has the amount of 50 kg/h pure water. What is the flow of solute inside the whole?

    The answer and its reason

    100 × 0.20 = 20 kg/h From A, and50 × 0 = 0 From B. Total 20 kg/h. This is the number you will use to balance the component in the next lesson.
